After a flash/close with lines dropped, the first open raises DTR+RTS together; the transition into DTR=1,RTS=1 resets the board (garbled ROM output, then READY); from that steady state, open/close with `open_port()` (HUPCL cleared, so the lines stay up) did NOT reset it in 6 cycles and `temp` was answered at the first attempt. Opening with the lines dropped (after esptool or a tool that leaves HUPCL set) resets the board once, harmless before a load. Any other tool that sets HUPCL and drops the lines at its close makes the NEXT open reset the board: during a run NOBODY may open the port with another tool. A9 therefore holds only in this steady state; proposed spec wording for A9/AC15: 'opening the port from the steady state does not reset; the first open after the lines were dropped resets once; during a run no other program may open the port'. Not applied to spec.md.
